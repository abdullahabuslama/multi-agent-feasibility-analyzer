## Order-to-delivery process
1. Customer places an order (app or web).
2. Payment is confirmed, or the order is marked cash on delivery.
3. The warehouse picks and packs within 4 hours for Express orders.
4. The order is handed to our own fleet (main cities) or a courier partner.
5. The customer gets SMS and WhatsApp updates at each step.

## Customer support process
- **Tier 1** agents handle common requests. The top three reasons for
  contact are: "Where is my order?" (38% of tickets), returns and refunds
  (22%), and payment problems (12%).
- **Tier 2** leads handle escalations, complaints, and compensation.
- About 38,000 tickets per month. First response target: under 2 minutes
  on chat, under 4 hours on email.
- CSAT in 2025: 84% (target 85%).
- Ticket volume grows about 2.5x during White Friday. Temporary agents
  are hired every November.

## How new projects are approved
1. The sponsoring department writes a one-page idea summary.
2. A feasibility study is prepared: technical, financial, market, and risk.
3. The Project Review Board (CEO, CFO, CTO, COO) decides.
4. Projects above EGP 2 million need CFO sign-off on the financial case.
5. Projects that touch customer personal data need a review by the Data
   Protection Officer (DPO) before launch.
6. New ideas usually start as a pilot of 3 months with clear success
   metrics before a full rollout.
