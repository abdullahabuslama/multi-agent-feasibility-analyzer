| Mobile phones & accessories | 31% | Highest traffic, lowest margin (8–10%) |

| Home & kitchen | 15% | Mostly marketplace sellers |
| Beauty & personal care | 11% | Fastest growing (+40% year on year) |

## Customer services
- **NileCart Express**: next-day delivery in Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria.
- **NileCart Plus**: subscription at EGP 99 per month. Free delivery and
  early access to campaigns. About 85,000 paying members.
- **Returns**: 14 days for most products. About 4.5% of orders are returned.
- **Installation service** for large appliances, done by partner technicians.

## Payment options (share of orders)
| Method | Share |
|---|---|
| Cash on delivery (COD) | 46% |
| Debit / credit cards | 31% |
| Mobile wallets | 13% |
| Buy now, pay later (BNPL) | 10% |

COD is popular but expensive: failed deliveries are higher for COD orders
(9%) than for prepaid orders (3%).

## Customer support channels
- Phone hotline (09:00–23:00).
- Live chat in the app and website.
- WhatsApp Business.
- Email (slowest channel, used mainly for complaints).

Arabic (Egyptian dialect) is used in more than 90% of conversations.

## Marketplace seller services
- Seller Center web portal for listings, orders, and payouts.
- Fulfilled-by-NileCart: sellers can store stock in our warehouses.
- Weekly payouts to sellers.
