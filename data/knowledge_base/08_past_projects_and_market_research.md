# NileCart — Past Projects and Market Research

> Synthetic document for training. NileCart is a fictional company.

## Past projects
### Help desk migration (2023)
- Moved phone, chat, and email support into one SaaS help desk tool.
- Took 4 months, cost EGP 3.2 million (budget EGP 2.8 million).
- CSAT rose from 78% to 84%. Average handling time fell by 15%.
- Lesson: data migration and agent training took longer than planned.

### Rule-based WhatsApp bot pilot (2024)
- A menu-based bot answered "Where is my order?" questions on WhatsApp.
- Handled 18% of those questions without an agent during the 3-month pilot.
- Stopped because customers wrote in free Egyptian Arabic and the menu
  frustrated them. CSAT for bot conversations was 61%.
- Lesson: customers expect to type freely; any new bot must understand
  Egyptian dialect and hand over to a human smoothly.

### Product recommendations (2024)
- "Customers also bought" recommendations on product pages and in the app.
- Average order value rose 4.5% for sessions that saw recommendations.
- Took 7 months instead of 5; final cost 30% above budget because of
  data-cleaning work.

### Cash-on-delivery confirmation calls (2025)
- Agents called customers before shipping high-value COD orders.
- Failed deliveries for those orders fell from 9% to 5%, but the calls
  used about 6 agents full time.

## Internal market research (2025 summary)
- Main competition: large regional marketplaces, global marketplaces
  operating in Egypt, and electronics retail chains with online stores.
- Customers choose mainly on price, delivery speed, and trust in
  genuine products.
- In our customer survey (n = 2,400), 57% said slow answers from customer
  support were their top frustration after delivery delays.
- 63% of surveyed customers said they would use a chat assistant if it
  could solve the problem without waiting for an agent.
- Competitor offerings change quickly; refresh this research with current
  public information before using it in a decision.
