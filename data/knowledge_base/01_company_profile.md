# NileCart — Company Profile

> Synthetic document for training. NileCart is a fictional company.

## Who we are
NileCart S.A.E. is an Egyptian online retailer founded in Cairo in 2017.
We sell electronics, home appliances, home & kitchen products, and beauty
products through our website and mobile apps.

## Key facts (fiscal year 2025)
- Gross merchandise value (GMV): EGP 4.2 billion.
- Active customers (ordered in the last 12 months): 1.4 million.
- Orders: about 120,000 per month on average; peaks of 6x normal traffic
  during the November "White Friday" campaign.
- Employees: 650 (including 70 engineers and 45 customer support agents).
- Coverage: Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria with next-day delivery; 20 other
  governorates with 2–5 day delivery.
- Mobile apps produce 68% of orders; the website produces 32%.

## Business model
1. First-party retail: we buy stock and sell it. Gross margin 12–18%
   depending on category.
2. Marketplace (since 2021): about 1,800 third-party sellers list products.
   We earn a commission of 8–15% per sale.
3. Delivery fees and the NileCart Plus subscription.
4. On-site advertising for brands and marketplace sellers.

## Strategy 2026–2028
- Grow the marketplace share of GMV from 35% to 50%.
- Make delivery faster and cheaper outside the three main cities.
- Reduce the cost of customer support while keeping customer satisfaction
  (CSAT) at or above 85%.
- Use data and AI where they show a clear, measurable return.
- Expand payment options, including more buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) partners.
